## 📱 Core Features

### Media Playback
- ✅ Audio playback (MP3, WAV, FLAC, AAC, OGG, M4A, WMA)
- ✅ Video playback (MP4, MKV, WebM, AVI, 3GP, FLV)

- ✅ Repeat modes (off, repeat all, repeat one)
